## The workflow we found

Intake happened on carbon-copy paper tickets. Status lived in three places: the ticket, a whiteboard, and the owner's memory. Customers called to ask "is it ready?" about thirty times a day.

## What we built

A small intake-and-status application shaped exactly like the paper ticket the team already knew — same fields, same order — plus an automatic "it's ready" text message to the customer.

## What changed

- Status calls dropped from ~30 a day to a handful.
- The whiteboard went back to being a whiteboard.
- Nothing about how the team worked had to change — the software matched the ticket, not the reverse.
