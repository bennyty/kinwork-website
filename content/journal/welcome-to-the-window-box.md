Every small business we've met runs on a workflow that no off-the-shelf product ever anticipated. A whiteboard that everyone glances at on the way in. A spreadsheet with twelve years of institutional knowledge in its column headers. A text thread that is, functionally, the dispatch system.

That workflow isn't a problem to be fixed. It's the accumulated wisdom of people who know their business better than any software vendor ever will.

## Software that moves in with you

Kinwork is a portmanteau of *kinship* and *working*. We sit with your team, learn how the work actually flows, and then design a bespoke application around it. The goal is software that feels like it has always been there — a tool that fits your hand, not a system you have to be trained into.

## What you'll find here

In this journal we'll publish two kinds of pieces:

- **Case studies** — real projects with the small businesses we work with: what their workflow looked like, what we built, and what changed.
- **Articles** — our thinking on custom software, workflow automation, and running a calmer, more efficient business.
